Agentic AI V4 — Batch 117
External Anchor Service Conformance and Cross-Store Recovery Protocol

Scope
- Validate that an external trust-anchor provider behaves according to the monotonic compare-and-set contract before a deployment relies on it.
- Persist a local operation journal around trust-registry changes that also require an external anchor update.
- Recover provable partial commits without replaying the local trust transition, silently recreating an anchor, or automatically aborting an operation whose runner may still be active.

Implementation
- Added `apps/agentic/backend/external-trust-anchor-conformance.js` with `runExternalTrustAnchorProviderConformance(provider, { ownerId, allowWrites })`.
- The conformance probe verifies an empty disposable namespace, genesis CAS, rejection of stale create requests, monotonic revision/sequence advancement, concurrent CAS with exactly one winner, rejection of stale-digest replay, rejection of sequence jumps, and owner-scope isolation.
- The probe is intentionally write-capable and requires `allowWrites: true` plus an owner ID with the reserved `__agentic_v4_conformance__` prefix. It writes up to three revisions into that namespace. Use only a disposable test tenant/namespace; never point it at a production owner. A passing behavioral probe cannot prove remote durability, operator separation, or resistance to whole-service rollback.
- Trust rotation/revocation now writes an owner-scoped `recovery-reconciliation-attestation-trust-transition-journal` before changing the local trust policy. Journal phases are hash-linked and digest-validated: `prepared`, `local_committed`, `challenge_applied`, `anchor_pending`, `completed`, or `blocked`.
- The journal binds the transition ID, challenge record, action/key, payload digest, expected policy revision/digest, resulting local revision/digest/event hash, and external anchor outcome. Journal creation is idempotent for the same prepared intent and rejects a conflicting binding.
- Added read-only journal status inspection and explicit cross-store recovery. Recovery requires exactly one trust-history event with the expected transition ID, payload digest, action/key, and next revision. It can finish the challenge record, advance a verified ancestor anchor with CAS, and close a journal when the anchor is current.
- A missing local event for a `prepared` journal is reported as `awaiting_local_commit`. Even after its age threshold, it is not automatically aborted: elapsed time does not prove the original runner is quiescent. No local trust transition is replayed by recovery.
- Recovery refuses corrupt journals, mismatched transition bindings, unhealthy/truncated trust audits, invalid/missing/ahead anchors, and conflicting state. It does not roll back the local registry or lower the external anchor. Concurrent recovery is idempotent and a completed journal cannot be downgraded by a stale recovery attempt.
- Added authenticated endpoints for bounded journal status and explicit recovery under `/api/agentic/recovery-audit/mutation-outcome-ledger/attestation-trust/cross-store-recovery`.

Operational procedure
1. Deploy the external anchor service independently and review its durable, append-only, atomic-CAS guarantees.
2. Run the conformance probe only against a dedicated disposable namespace. Confirm all checks pass before enabling the provider for real owners.
3. Configure `AGENTIC_TRUST_ANCHOR_URL` and `AGENTIC_TRUST_ANCHOR_TOKEN`, or inject a provider implementing `read(ownerId)` and `compareAndSet(ownerId, expectedDigest, candidate)`.
4. Inspect `GET .../attestation-trust/cross-store-recovery` after trust-sensitive operations. A `recovery_required` status indicates a prepared or incomplete cross-store transition.
5. If the provider failed after local commit, restore provider availability and call `POST .../attestation-trust/cross-store-recovery/recover`. Recovery advances only an external anchor that is a verified ancestor of the local history and only after a complete healthy local trust audit.
6. If a journal remains `prepared` without a matching local history event, recovery reports the condition and does not automatically abort it. Determine runner quiescence and challenge validity out of band before taking any further action.
7. If an anchor is ahead of local state, mismatched, invalid, or missing for a non-genesis registry, do not overwrite or reset it. Restore from a verified source or use a separately authorized trust-root recovery process.

Operational boundaries
- The conformance probe is a behavioral check, not a cryptographic proof of the remote service's storage durability or resistance to administrator rollback.
- The local operation journal and local trust registry share the application's storage boundary; the external anchor remains a separate store. Cross-store atomic transactions are not claimed.
- A stale prepared journal is intentionally not auto-aborted because the system has no independent runner-quiescence attestation for that transition.
- The HTTP listener was not startup-tested in this validation environment; endpoint syntax/mapping and cumulative tests were validated, but live HTTP boot is not claimed.
