Agentic AI V4 — Batch 202

Objective:
Make freshness generations explicitly supersede predecessors, require independently auditable continuity for drift-resolution receipts, and persist signed proof of each recovery-gate decision.

Scope:
- Append signed supersession events binding the prior freshness attestation to a replacement generation.
- Resume an interrupted supersession using the same operation ID and reconcile its external recovery-receipt checkpoint before issuing the replacement attestation.
- Audit supersession history for replay, conflicting targets, invalid signatures, missing replacement generations, and predecessor ordering.
- Append a signed drift-resolution continuity receipt after each signed drift-resolution event.
- Keep drift evidence unresolved until the continuity receipt is present and the external recovery-receipt checkpoint covers it.
- Resume interrupted drift-resolution/continuity pairs without duplicate events.
- Persist a signed, CAS-protected recovery-gate proof ledger bound to checkpoint, freshness, lifecycle, drift, closure, receipt-checkpoint, and key-lifecycle state.
- Revalidate stored proof against current evidence; a changed anchor, expired freshness attestation, key cutoff, or changed closure digest makes the proof stale.
- Expose read-only supersession and recovery-gate proof audit endpoints.

Operational invariants:
- A predecessor freshness attestation cannot be used for current decisions after a signed supersession event.
- A supersession without a replacement remains pending and blocks current-decision use.
- Drift resolution is not complete until its continuity receipt is signed, persisted, and covered by the current external recovery-receipt checkpoint.
- A recovery-gate proof of a blocked decision is auditable but never decision-usable.
- Proof replay with identical inputs is idempotent; changed bindings require a distinct proof generation.
- External anchors are never rewritten to conceal rollback.
- Private signing keys are not distributed in source archives.
