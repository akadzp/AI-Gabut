Agentic AI V4 Roadmap — Batch 142

Objective
---------
Close the verification gap between root-recovery trust provider declarations and observed CAS behavior, make freshness requirements explicit at current-decision boundaries, and provide safe reconciliation for retained provider-conformance drift evidence.

Scope delivered
---------------
1. Challenge-bound conformance probe for the root-recovery trust anchor provider, including disposable namespace, contract metadata, CAS, read-after-write, stale-CAS non-mutation, monotonicity, owner binding, and concurrent-writer checks.
2. Digest-bound retention and audit of conformance reports for the `root_recovery_trust` domain.
3. Decision-boundary gate for signed retention-reconciliation attestations. The gate requires valid signature and retention, optional expected-snapshot binding, and freshness under both signed and local policy limits.
4. Drift-retention chain reconciliation with record/tombstone integrity preflight, CAS-based chain reconciliation, and post-reconciliation audits.
5. Authenticated application APIs and HTTP routes for these operations.

Security invariants
-------------------
- Conformance writes only to a unique disposable namespace and does not mutate production root-trust state.
- A completed challenge is replay-idempotent; an uncompleted challenge requires matching digest-bound confirmation and an unexpired challenge.
- A stale attestation may remain historically verifiable but is never accepted by the explicit current-decision gate.
- Drift event payloads and retained-evidence records must match their digests before reconciliation.
- Chain reconciliation never rewrites local evidence or bypasses rollback, mismatch, or corrupt-ledger findings.
- All operations remain owner-scoped and read-only audits do not mutate state.

Known limitations
-----------------
