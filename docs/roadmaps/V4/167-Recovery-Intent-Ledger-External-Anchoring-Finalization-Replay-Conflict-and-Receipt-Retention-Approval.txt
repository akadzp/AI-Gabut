Agentic AI V4 — Batch 167 Roadmap
=================================

Scope
-----
1. Anchor immutable recovery-intent records from the key-transition receipt, recovery-intent attestation lifecycle, manifest gap-finalization, and signed verification-receipt recovery flows in a dedicated external snapshot checkpoint.
2. Validate each intent family through its schema-specific audit before including it in the external recovery-intent snapshot; do not confuse legacy checkpoint-service intents with digest-chained marker-recovery intents.
3. Add explicit checkpoint enrollment, freshness/status audit, and one-event continuity reconciliation. Preserve history-not-proven semantics after first enrollment.
4. Make finalization checkpoint recovery replay-aware: idempotently recognize completed recovery, finalize a pending intent when the external anchor already proves the exact postcondition, reject conflicting unresolved intents, and detect marker rollback after a completed recovery.
5. Add a challenge-bound retention review for signed verification receipts. Bind exact receipt IDs/digests, source snapshot digest/count, external checkpoint digest/sequence, and policy digest. Revalidate all bindings before applying `retain` or `escalate`.
6. Keep retention review non-destructive. No evidence deletion or compaction is permitted.
7. Expose authenticated application APIs and HTTP routes and add focused regression tests.

Security invariants
-------------------
- Owner scoping is mandatory for checkpoint, intent, challenge, approval, and receipt access.
- The external recovery-intent snapshot includes immutable intent bindings but excludes mutable status fields; schema-specific audits remain mandatory.
- Recovery under strict anchoring requires an anchored, fresh external recovery-intent checkpoint. First enrollment's `historyNotProven` flag remains intact and blocks current-decision use.
- Ordinary recovery-intent checkpoint reconciliation advances only one provable event. Multi-event gaps are not silently combined.
- A completed finalization recovery cannot be replayed as a new transition. If its marker disappears afterward, recovery fails closed as rollback.
- Receipt retention approval is valid only while its challenge, source snapshot, target receipts, checkpoint, and policy bindings remain current.
- `retain` resolves only the bound review-due targets; `escalate` remains explicit and does not authorize current-decision use.
- No receipt, approval, challenge, or evidence is deleted or compacted.
