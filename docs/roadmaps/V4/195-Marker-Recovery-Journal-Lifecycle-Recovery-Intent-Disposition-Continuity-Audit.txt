Objective
Add explicit, auditable disposition for marker-recovery journal lifecycle recovery intents that cannot safely resume, and independently verify the intent/lifecycle/finalization chain.

Delivered
1. Signed append-only quarantine and mark_unrecoverable disposition events.
2. Signed disposition finalization receipts with checkpoint binding and key-cutoff evidence.
3. Deterministic target-scoped operation IDs and CAS-safe idempotent recovery.

5. Closure enforcement: disposition reduces operational ambiguity but does not restore current-decision eligibility.

Safety invariants
- Never delete or rewrite historical lifecycle evidence.
- Do not disposition a healthy, current, fully finalized lifecycle intent.
- Require explicit confirmation and a bounded challenge lifetime.
- Reject key rotation between challenge preparation and first disposition commit.
- If a disposition event has already committed, permit idempotent recovery of its checkpoint/finalization chain without duplicating receipts.
- Treat key-cutoff and dispositioned records as stale for current-decision use.
- Fail closed on duplicate operation IDs, invalid signatures, broken sequence ordering, missing finalization records, or orphaned events.
