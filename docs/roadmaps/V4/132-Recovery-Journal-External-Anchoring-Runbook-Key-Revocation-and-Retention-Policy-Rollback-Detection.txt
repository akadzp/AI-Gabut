Batch 132 — Recovery Journal External Anchoring, Runbook Key Revocation and Compromise Cutoff, and Retention Policy Rollback Detection

Scope
- Add a dedicated external anchor trust domain for contract-migration recovery journals.
- Anchor the initial journal genesis before later transitions, then advance external journal anchors on every durable journal transition.
- Detect local journal rollback, missing anchors for journals with history, binding mismatch, invalid anchor payloads, CAS conflicts, and provider outages. Reconciliation advances only a verified local descendant and refuses to overwrite unresolved trust conflicts.
- Add recovery-authority-signed revocation challenges for runbook signing keys, optional compromise cutoffs, and historical receipt verification that distinguishes signatures made before the cutoff from signatures at/after it.
- Permit replacement-key recovery after the active runbook key has been revoked, requiring a separately configured recovery authority signature and new-key proof of possession.
- Add a retention-policy high-water checkpoint that detects rollback and same-revision substitution and blocks later retention-policy changes after a detected conflict.
- Expose authenticated application APIs and HTTP routes for journal anchor status/anchor/reconciliation/audit and runbook key revocation. Retention rollback is surfaced through the existing retention policy audit API.

Operational contract
- Configure a dedicated provider with `AGENTIC_RECOVERY_JOURNAL_ANCHOR_URL` and `AGENTIC_RECOVERY_JOURNAL_ANCHOR_TOKEN`; enable strict enforcement with `AGENTIC_REQUIRE_RECOVERY_JOURNAL_ANCHOR=true` or `requireRecoverySloRecoveryJournalAnchor: true`.
- The recovery-journal anchor provider must be independent from both the general attestation trust anchor and the finalization key-policy anchor. Provider instance reuse and duplicate configured environment URLs are rejected.
- Supply `recoverySloRunbookRecoveryPublicKeys` as a registry of Ed25519 recovery-authority public keys. Recovery authority keys must not reuse an operational runbook signing public key. Keep recovery private keys outside application storage.
- For compromise revocation, `invalidateBefore` is an ISO-compatible timestamp no later than the current clock. Runbook signatures issued at or after the cutoff are rejected; pre-cutoff signatures remain eligible for historical verification unless otherwise invalidated.
- Retention checkpoint enrollment on first observation of a pre-existing policy is marked `legacy_enrollment`; it cannot prove the policy was never rolled back before that first enrollment. Subsequent rollback and substitution are detected while the checkpoint remains intact.

Known limits
- Recovery-journal anchors bind the latest journal revision but the provider interface does not expose complete external anchor history. The `previousAnchorDigest` link is shape-checked but cannot be independently traversed without a historical provider API.
- A local retention checkpoint is not an external anchor. Restoring both the retention policy and its checkpoint from the same old snapshot is outside the detection guarantee.
- External provider durability, administrator separation, and resistance to rollback of the provider itself must be guaranteed by the independently operated anchor service.
