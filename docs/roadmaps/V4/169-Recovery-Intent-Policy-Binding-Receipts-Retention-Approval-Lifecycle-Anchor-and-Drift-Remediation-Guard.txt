Agentic AI V4 Roadmap — Batch 169
=================================

Objective
---------
Close the Batch 168 gaps around proof of policy-binding state, independent external anchoring of receipt-retention approval lifecycle, and guarded cross-ledger drift remediation.

Deliverables
------------
1. Policy-binding verification receipt:
   - Bind owner, checkpoint domain, source digest/count, anchor digest/sequence, policy digests, freshness, and policy-binding approval-chain digest.
   - Verify digest integrity and compare the recorded binding with current checkpoint state.
   - Distinguish historical validity from current usability; do not treat pre-enrollment history as proven.
2. Retention approval lifecycle external checkpoint:
   - Independently anchor retention-review challenge, approval, revocation-challenge, and revocation events.
   - Preserve immutable event identity when a prepared challenge is finalized in place.
   - Require one-event continuity proof before normal reconciliation; fail closed on policy drift, ambiguity, source corruption, and CAS conflicts.
3. Cross-ledger drift remediation plan/execution guard:
   - Prepare a short-lived plan bound to a stable cross-ledger state fingerprint.
   - Whitelist only safe single-event checkpoint reconciliation actions with unique continuity proof.
   - Revalidate state immediately before execution; reject stale plans and action substitution.
   - Persist execution evidence for idempotent replay and never delete/compact evidence.
4. Authenticated application API and HTTP route integration.
