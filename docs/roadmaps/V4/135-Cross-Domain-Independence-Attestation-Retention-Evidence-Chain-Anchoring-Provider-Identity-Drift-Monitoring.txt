Batch 135 — Cross-Domain Independence Attestation, Retention Evidence Chain Anchoring, and Provider Identity Drift Monitoring

Scope
1. Sign cross-domain anchor-independence reports using Ed25519 and verify them against an independent trusted public-key registry.
2. Retain signed reports as evidence and verify their signatures during evidence audits.
3. Add a persistent per-owner append-only retained-evidence hash chain for retain/prune transitions.
4. Support a distinct optional/required external CAS anchor for the retained-evidence chain, including post-write verification, sequence continuity, and rollback/removal detection.
5. Establish provider identity baselines only through digest-bound, expiring operator challenges.
6. Detect identity drift and reject baseline application if identities changed after the challenge was prepared.
7. Expose owner-scoped application APIs and HTTP routes for attestation, chain status/audit/reconciliation, and drift baseline management.

Acceptance criteria
- Signatures are validated before an attestation is retained.
- Attestation verification distinguishes signature integrity from approval and does not overclaim infrastructure independence.
- Retained evidence and tombstone transitions are bound to hash-linked chain events.
- External chain anchor rollback, missing anchor after enrollment, digest mismatch, and provider outage fail closed for pruning.
- Identity baseline is not auto-created or auto-updated.
- Baseline challenge replay, expiry, stale baseline, and identity snapshot drift are rejected.
- Owner isolation and route/API mapping are tested.
- Full cumulative tests and archive integrity checks pass.

Known limitations
- External anchoring is optional for compatibility; strict deployments must enable it.
- The provider identity audit observes declared endpoint/backend identities and cannot prove independent administrative control of remote infrastructure.
- Local baseline and chain storage cannot detect rollback if all local state is restored together and no independent anchor is configured.
