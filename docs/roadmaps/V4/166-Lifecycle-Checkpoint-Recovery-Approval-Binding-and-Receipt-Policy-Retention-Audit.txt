Security invariants
-------------------

- No marker repair if policy digest or previous-anchor continuity cannot be proven.
- No finalization checkpoint recovery without a healthy approval-bound finalization ledger.
- No deletion or compaction as a side effect of retention audit.
- Initial enrollment history remains explicitly unproven.
