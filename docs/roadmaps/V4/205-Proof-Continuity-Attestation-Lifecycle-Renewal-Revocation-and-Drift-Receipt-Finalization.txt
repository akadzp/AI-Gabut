Batch 205 — Proof-Continuity Attestation Lifecycle Renewal and Revocation, Drift-Resolution Receipt Finalization, and Cross-Generation Replay Enforcement

Objectives
1. Add challenge-bound renewal and terminal revocation for proof-continuity attestations.
2. Maintain signed append-only intent, applied, supersession, and finalization evidence.
3. Bind supersession to the predecessor and replacement generation and reject cross-generation replay.
4. Finalize each drift-resolution continuity event with a separate signed receipt anchored by the existing independent continuity checkpoint.
5. Resume durable operations after local commit/external checkpoint interruption without duplicating events.
6. Preserve historical completion after key cutoff while blocking stale lifecycle evidence from current-decision use.

Acceptance criteria
- A renewal produces a monotonic generation and an auditable predecessor/replacement binding.
- A revoked target is terminal and cannot be renewed.
- An operation with a durable intent resumes after challenge expiry or transient external-anchor failure.
- No drift-resolution event is considered fully finalized while its receipt is missing or unanchored.
- Signature, digest-chain, sequence, challenge, generation, checkpoint, and signing-key bindings are independently audited.
- Relevant focused regression tests, syntax checks, import resolution, archive integrity, and post-extraction tests pass.
- Source ZIP is cumulative; no GitHub commit is performed.
