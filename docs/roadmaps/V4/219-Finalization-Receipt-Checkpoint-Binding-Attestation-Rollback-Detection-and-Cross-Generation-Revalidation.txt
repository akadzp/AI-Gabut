Finalization-Receipt Checkpoint Binding Attestation, Rollback Detection, and Cross-Generation Recovery-Gate Revalidation

Scope
- Sign an immutable binding for the independent finalization-receipt checkpoint.
- Bind external anchor sequence/digest, snapshot/source digest and count, receipt evidence digest, and signing-key lifecycle state.
- Verify predecessor-linked attestation generations and detect rollback/fork evidence.
- Integrate the audit result into recovery-gate convergence and generation-binding digest.
- Expose attestation and audit operations through the existing authenticated proof-continuity API.

Acceptance criteria
- Identical binding requests are idempotent.
- A changed but current checkpoint binding requires a new generation.
- A stale or revoked upstream receipt cannot become current solely because a checkpoint binding attestation exists.
- Anchor rollback or same-sequence digest mismatch makes the audit unhealthy.
- Strict enforcement is opt-in and blocks current-decision use while required attestation is missing or stale.
- Source syntax, imports, focused regressions, and ZIP integrity are validated after extraction.
