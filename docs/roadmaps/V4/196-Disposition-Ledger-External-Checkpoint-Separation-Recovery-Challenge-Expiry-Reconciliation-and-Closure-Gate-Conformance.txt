Agentic AI V4 — Batch 196 Roadmap Record

Objective
Separate marker-recovery journal lifecycle disposition evidence into its own external checkpoint trust domain, reconcile challenge expiry according to whether a disposition commit is durable, and enforce closure decision-gate conformance.

Delivered
1. Dedicated external snapshot checkpoint for signed lifecycle disposition and disposition-finalization events.
2. Independent provider/domain registration and provider-reuse visibility in trust-domain independence audit.
3. Enrollment, status, audit, reconciliation, and HTTP endpoints for the new checkpoint.
4. Disposition challenge binding to checkpoint anchor digest, sequence, and freshness snapshot.
5. Pre-commit fail-closed checks and idempotent external CAS recovery after a durable disposition event.
6. Expiry reconciliation: expire an uncommitted challenge without creating an event; resume a durable commit even if the challenge window elapsed after commit.
7. Independent continuity audit and closure gate block use of stale/unanchored disposition evidence.
8. Deterministic operation IDs, signed append-only events, CAS finalization, and no duplicate disposition/finalization receipts on retry.

Safety invariants
- Do not silently enroll pre-existing disposition history.
- Do not share the new anchor provider with any other trust domain.
- Do not create a disposition event from an expired challenge that has no durable commit.
- If a disposition event is durable, resume the same chain instead of generating a replacement challenge.
- Fail closed on invalid signature, changed challenge/checkpoint binding, stale checkpoint, rollback, or unproven multi-event gap.
- A completed disposition does not restore current-decision eligibility.
