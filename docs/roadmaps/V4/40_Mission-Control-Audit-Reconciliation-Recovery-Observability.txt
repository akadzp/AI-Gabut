V4 Batch 43 — Mission Control Audit Reconciliation Recovery Observability

Scope:
- emit durable evidence when an explicit audit-reconciliation recovery action completes
- persist recovery-evidence intent before emission so evidence failure never erases the completed recovery
- retry pending recovery evidence without rerunning the underlying reconciliation repair
- use deterministic reconciliation-scoped dedupe keys and bounded retries
- preserve owner scoping, concurrency safety, reconciliation authority, and execution authority

DoD: an explicit recovery action that clears pending audit reconciliation state produces an idempotent owner-scoped recovery-completed timeline event; if evidence emission fails, the durable recovery remains complete and the evidence can be retried without repeating the repair.
