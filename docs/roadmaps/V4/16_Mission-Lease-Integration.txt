V4 Batch 16 — Mission Execution Lease Integration

Objective:
Make the Batch 15 execution lease an actual scheduler boundary rather than a standalone primitive.

Scope:
- persist missions and mission leases in the agentic store
- acquire an owner-scoped lease before a mission stage executes
- propagate missionId/executionId into the Work runner
- release the lease after the stage result is persisted
- reject concurrent execution while an active lease exists
- keep stage advancement and approval authority unchanged
