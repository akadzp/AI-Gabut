V4 — OBSERVABILITY & WORK HISTORY

Tujuan: menyediakan history pekerjaan yang berguna bagi user dan
operator tanpa menyimpan hidden chain-of-thought.

Scope:
- work timeline
- execution timeline
- action history
- approval history
- change history
- verification history
- failure / retry history
- result history
- operational correlation
- search / filtering

DoD: User dapat merekonstruksi lifecycle work dari observable events,
state, changes, approvals, dan evidence.
