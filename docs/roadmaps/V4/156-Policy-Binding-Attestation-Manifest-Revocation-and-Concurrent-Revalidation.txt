Agentic AI V4 — Batch 156 Roadmap

Objective

Bind policy-binding audit checkpoint freshness to signed evidence, make manifest approval supersession/revocation explicit and auditable, and ensure overlapping retention-decision revalidation requests cannot independently publish conflicting outcomes.

Implemented in Batch 156

1. Policy-binding audit checkpoint freshness attestation
   - Ed25519 signature and trusted-key verification.
   - Snapshot digest includes the active max-age and future-skew policy.
   - Historical signature validity is distinct from currentness and current-decision usability.
   - Unproven pre-enrollment history remains visible and cannot authorize a current decision.

2. Manifest approval supersession and revocation
   - Read-only audit derives superseded status when the approved manifest no longer matches the current source/checkpoint state.
   - Revocation requires a ten-minute challenge bound to approval, manifest, and the exact observed stale source state.
   - Source changes or restoration of eligibility invalidate the challenge.
   - Revocation is recorded separately; the original approval is immutable and no compaction/deletion is executed.

3. Concurrent revalidation consistency
   - Per-process single-flight coordination coalesces overlapping calls for the same owner and approval.
   - Generation conflicts remain fail-closed and are not cached.
   - Expired checkpoint freshness is reported as pending rather than misclassified as a source race.
   - Cache observability includes coalesced and in-flight counts.

Known limitations

- In-flight coalescing is process-local; multi-process coordination depends on shared store/provider CAS semantics.
- The revalidation cache is in-memory and not an authority source.
- Attestations for unproven legacy history are historical-only.
- Manifest approval revocation does not execute compaction or deletion.
- HTTP listener startup remains outside the verified test boundary.
