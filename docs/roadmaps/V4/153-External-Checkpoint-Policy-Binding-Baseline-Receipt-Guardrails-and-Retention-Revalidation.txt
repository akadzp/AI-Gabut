Agentic AI V4 — Batch 153
External Checkpoint Freshness Policy Binding, Baseline Receipt Recovery/Compaction Guardrails, and Retention Decision Evidence Revalidation

Scope
-----
1. Bind the active freshness/future-skew/retention policy to cross-domain external checkpoint anchors with a canonical domain-scoped policy digest.
2. Detect policy drift separately from source drift and require explicit, time-limited policy-binding approval before appending a checkpoint under changed policy.
3. Recover interrupted policy-binding finalization only when the external anchor proves the exact expected one-step append; reject stale challenge or source changes.
4. Provide read-only provider-baseline transition receipt recovery and compaction guardrails. No destructive compaction occurs.
5. Revalidate retention-review decisions against the approval, challenge, replay guard, target evidence digests, current status, and external checkpoint/policy binding.

Security invariants
-------------------
- Historical checkpoint integrity does not imply current policy conformance.
- Policy drift blocks current-decision use and ordinary reconciliation until an explicit binding transition succeeds.
- Policy-binding challenges are owner/domain/source/anchor/policy bound and expire after ten minutes.
- Source changes invalidate an outstanding policy-binding challenge.
- Baseline receipt compaction remains prohibited automatically; eligibility is only advisory and requires healthy source audits, a current external checkpoint, no pending transition, and at least two verified receipts.
- Retention approval cannot authorize current use when target evidence is missing, modified, superseded, replay protection is invalid, or the required external checkpoint is not current.
- No evidence is deleted, rewritten, or compacted by Batch 153.
